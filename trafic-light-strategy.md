Traffic Light Setup
1.⁠ ⁠The Core Setup (Finding the Pair)
The foundational rule of this strategy is to look for a specific pair of consecutive candlesticks: one red candle and one green candle (the order does not matter; it can be red-green or green-red).
•⁠  ⁠Once you spot this pair, mark the highest point (top wick) and the lowest point (bottom wick) of these two candles to establish your trading range.
•⁠  ⁠Entry: If the price breaks above the high of this range, you buy a Call Option (CE). If it breaks below the low of the range, you buy a Put Option (PE).
2.⁠ ⁠Timeframes and Rules
The strategy utilizes different timeframes depending on the trading style and market conditions:
A. 1-Minute Chart (For Scalping)
•⁠  ⁠Ignore the first candle: You must ignore the very first minute candle of the day (9:15 AM to 9:16 AM) due to high morning volatility. Start looking for your red/green pair from the second candle onwards.
•⁠  ⁠Two Trades Limit: You are only allowed a maximum of two trades on the 1-minute chart. If your stop loss (SL) is hit twice, the 1-minute chart becomes invalid for the rest of the day, and you must switch to the 5-minute chart.
B. 5-Minute Chart (For Intraday)
•⁠  ⁠Do not ignore the first candle: Unlike the 1-minute chart, you consider every candle on the 5-minute chart, including the 9:15 AM one.
•⁠  ⁠100-Point Rule: If the point difference between the high and low of your pair (which acts as your stop loss) is more than 100 points, ignore that pair and wait for the next one. This ensures your risk-to-reward ratio is maintained.
C. 15-Minute Chart (For Options Selling/Positional)
•⁠  ⁠If you are an options seller aiming to capture slow premium decay (theta decay), use the 15-minute timeframe to find your pairs, as it provides a slower, steadier trend with less volatility.
3.⁠ ⁠Strike Price Selection
•⁠  ⁠For Scalping (Small Targets): Choose At-The-Money (ATM) strike prices, ideally with premiums ranging between Rs. 200 and Rs. 300. This ensures good profitability if the market moves quickly.
•⁠  ⁠For Capturing Big Trends: If you are anticipating a massive move (e.g., 400-500 points), choose Out-of-The-Money (OTM) options with premiums around Rs. 50 to Rs. 150. This allows you to hold the trade longer without being shaken out by heavy volatility and slippage.
4.⁠ ⁠Special Rule for Office Goers (Job Holders)
If you cannot watch the market at the 9:15 AM open, you can apply this exact strategy in the second half of the day. Start observing the 5-minute chart at 1:00 PM. Begin looking for your red-green pair from the 1:00 PM candle onwards and trade the breakout.
5.⁠ ⁠Risk Management
•⁠  ⁠Max 3 Stop Losses Per Day: You should not take more than three consecutive stop losses in a single day. If your SL hits three times in a row, it indicates a completely sideways market, and you should stop trading for the day
